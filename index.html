Privacy Policy

Dental Clinic app · Effective date: 14 October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Dental Clinic ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and shares your information when you use the Dental Clinic mobile application and its related online service (together, the "Service"). The Service connects patients, dentists and clinic administrators so they can find clinics, book appointments, communicate and leave reviews.

By creating an account or using the Service you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, and a password (stored only in hashed form, never in plain text).
  • Profile details: information you add to your patient, dentist or clinic profile, such as specialty, qualifications, clinic name, address, working hours, services and a profile photo.
  • Verification details for dentists and clinics: registration or licence information and supporting documents you upload so an administrator can review and approve your account.
  • Appointments: the clinic, dentist, date, time, service and any notes you add when booking, rescheduling or cancelling.
  • Messages: chat messages you send to other users of the Service (for example between a patient and a clinic).
  • Reviews and ratings you write about dentists or clinics.
  • Job and recruitment activity: vacancies you post, and applications or messages you send when applying to a clinic.
  • Support requests: anything you send us when you contact us.

Information created when you use the Service

  • One-time codes (OTP): when you sign up or reset a password we create a short-lived verification code and email it to you. Only a hashed version of the code is stored.
  • Notifications: records of notifications we send you about appointments, approvals and messages.
  • Technical data: basic request logs such as IP address, device and app version, and error information, used for security and to keep the Service running.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party advertising in the Service.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and verify your email address.
  • To let you search clinics and dentists, book and manage appointments, and chat with other users.
  • To review and approve dentist and clinic registrations and recruitment requests.
  • To send service emails and in-app notifications, such as verification codes, approvals and appointment updates.
  • To display reviews and ratings.
  • To protect the Service, prevent abuse and fraud, apply rate limits, and fix problems.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. Service providers we use

We share data only with providers that help us run the Service, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Hosting: our application server runs on Render (render.com).
  • Database: your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database provided by Supabase (supabase.com).
  • Email delivery: verification and service emails are sent through our email provider.

These providers process data on our behalf and may store it on servers outside your country. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the Service.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. Verification codes expire quickly and are invalidated after use.

How to delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time by emailing bilalbhatti.dev@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Some information may be kept for a limited time where we must keep it by law, or for security and fraud-prevention reasons. Reviews and messages that other users already received may remain visible to them in anonymised form.

7. Security

We protect your information with measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, hashed one-time codes, access controls, rate limiting and private storage for sensitive documents. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password private.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date (most details can be edited in your profile);
  • delete your account and data (see section 6);
  • object to or restrict certain uses of your information.

To use these rights, contact us using the details in section 11.

9. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. A parent or guardian may book appointments for a child using their own account.

10. Health information

The Service is a booking and communication tool. It is not a medical record system. Please do not send sensitive health details in chat or reviews unless it is necessary, and never use the Service for emergencies. If you have a dental or medical emergency, contact your local emergency services or a clinic directly.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at bilalbhatti.dev@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page and, for important changes, notify you in the app or by email. Continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
